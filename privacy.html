Architectural Transparency

How your images move through our studio

Every tool operates with a strict ephemeral lifecycle. From upload to export, files never touch persistent storage.

01

Encrypted Transport

When you submit an image, it streams directly via HTTPS TLS 1.3 encryption to an isolated volatile memory buffer on our conversion nodes. No intermediate proxies inspect your content.

02

In-Memory Transformation

The selected transformation executes strictly within volatile RAM: edge contour cutting, matrix texture upscaling, or dimension compression. Nothing is written to a physical hard drive.

03

Instant Stream & Purge

The output file is returned to your browser as a binary blob stream. The moment your browser finishes receiving the result, the temporary memory allocation is immediately released and purged.

Does Pixlab keep any copy or backup of my uploaded photos? +

No. Images are received in volatile server RAM buffer, processed, and streamed back to your browser. Once transmission completes, the volatile memory allocation is automatically wiped. We do not maintain any disk archive, backup gallery, or file cache.

Are customer photos used to train machine learning models? +

Never. We use pre-trained, static production models for background cutting and resolution scaling. User files are never harvested, pooled, or utilized for algorithmic training.

Can I use Pixlab for proprietary or NDA-protected client imagery? +

Yes. Because we do not store files, employ human reviewers, or share data with third parties, Pixlab is safe for commercial studios, agency lookbooks, and proprietary client product shots.

How do I request immediate deletion of my previous contact messages? +

Simply email support@pixlab.com or message us through our Contact page requesting the erasure of your email records. We process all verified deletion requests within 48 business hours.
