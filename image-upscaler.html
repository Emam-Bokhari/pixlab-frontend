Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about scale factors, edge clarity, file formats, and privacy.

Which scale multipliers are available?

You can choose between 2x, 3x, and 4x scale. For example, a 600 × 400 pixel image scales to 1200 × 800 at 2x, 1800 × 1200 at 3x, or 2400 × 1600 at 4x.

Will the enlarged image become blurry or pixelated?

The upscaling pipeline utilizes high-fidelity multi-pass interpolation designed to suppress blockiness, soften harsh pixel steps, and preserve clean contours across contrasting textures.

Which image formats are supported?

The upscaler accepts JPEG, JPG, PNG, WEBP, BMP, TIFF, and AVIF files up to 50 MB, outputting your high-resolution image in the exact original format.

Are uploaded photographs stored or made public?

No. All files are processed in volatile, ephemeral memory buffers and are never retained, cataloged, or shared. Everything is automatically cleared immediately after export.
