Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about our platform, features, privacy, and performance.

Q1 What is Pixlab and how does it process images?

Pixlab is a modern online image utility designed to remove backgrounds, upscale images up to 4x, compress to exact KB targets, and resize with smart cropping. Everything works through fast browser-accelerated processing to give you instant studio-grade results.

Q2 Do I need to sign up or create an account to use the tools?

No. Pixlab is built with zero friction in mind. All four core tools (Background Remover, Upscaler, Compressor, Resizer) are completely free to use directly in your browser without requiring passwords, credit cards, or email logins.

Q3 How does Pixlab guarantee the privacy of my uploaded photos?

Your media privacy is central to our engineering. Files uploaded for processing are handled in temporary volatile memory and are purged immediately after the output is downloaded. We do not store your images, sell your data, or use your private photos to train public models.

Q4 What scales and formats does the Image Upscaler support?

The upscaler supports 2x, 3x, and 4x enlargement for PNG, JPG, JPEG, WEBP, BMP, and TIFF files up to 50 MB. It sharpens fine boundaries and reconstructs high-resolution clarity without creating pixelated blocks.

Q5 Can I compress an image down to a specific target file size in KB?

Yes. Our Image Compressor lets you define an exact target limit (e.g. 200 KB or 500 KB), which is essential when submitting official documents, government portals, university admissions, and online marketplaces that enforce strict upload caps.

Q6 What should I do if I need enterprise volume or custom API access?

If your business processes thousands of images daily and requires automated batch workflows or custom headless API access, reach out to our team via our Contact page. We provide dedicated solutions for high-volume catalogs.
