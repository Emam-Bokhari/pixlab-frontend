Commercial Licensing & Client Resale

Any asset transformed via Pixlab may be utilized for unrestricted commercial initiatives. This includes, without limitation:

Clause 04

Studio Operational Boundaries & Capacities

To safeguard cluster availability and prevent memory starvation for all simultaneous creators, operational boundaries are established:

  • Payload Cap: A hard threshold of 50 Megabytes (MB) per single image submission applies across all tools.
  • Format Compliance: Accepted raster formats are limited to standard WebP, PNG, JPEG, and AVIF encodings.
  • Interactive Human Use: Tools are optimized for direct browser human interaction. Automated high-frequency scripts, unauthorized headless scrapers, or botnet submission floods are blocked by automated rate filters.
